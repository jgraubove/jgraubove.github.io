Model description

Psychrometrics
Standard thermodynamic relationships are used to compute vapour pressure, dew point, absolute humidity and enthalpy from temperature and relative humidity.
Conservation Heating
Calculates the temperature increase required to reduce Relative Humidity to 50% while maintaining constant absolute moisture content. Based on the inverse Magnus formula.
Basic Lifetime Multiplier
A relative rate model is used:

LM = kref / k

where k depends on temperature and humidity (Arrhenius-type formulation).
Strlič paper degradation model
Paper degradation follows a kinetic model:

k = exp(36.987 + humidity term + pH term − temperature term)

Lifetime is derived using the Ekenstam relationship.
Photographic materials
Empirical model:

k = exp(32 + 0.01 RH − 11000/(T+273.15))
Mould growth (Johansson)
The model combines temperature and humidity response functions derived from experimental curves. It is only activated above 80% RH and scaled to produce a 0–1 risk index.
PVC storage lifetime
Based on:

ln k = 34.2 − 10300/(T + 273.5) + constants + humidity term

Lifetime = 15 / exp(k)
